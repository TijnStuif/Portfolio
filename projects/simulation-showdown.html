Simulation Showdown

A 3D boss-fight game centred around a simulation that turns against the player.

Role

Gameplay Programmer

Team

4 people

Duration

12 weeks

Unity C# Git Agile/SCRUM

The Game

Simulation Showdown is my first ever big Unity game project. The game itself is a 3D boss-fight game, where the boss uses the environment and senses of the player against them.

My Contribution

  • Worked on the first version of player movement
  • Made most attacks + indicators
  • Created aesthetic (background, floor & environment)
  • Worked on implementing SFX and created my own music
  • Made cutscenes in the game (fight start, death & win)

What I Learned

I learned a lot about Unity since this was my first big project in the engine. Especially around the importance of good sound design and bringing it naturally. Furthermore, working on mechanics such as the movement & cutscenes learned me a lot about different parts of the Unity engine, that I have continued to use in my later projects.
